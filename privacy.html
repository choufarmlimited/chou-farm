Privacy Policy

How Chou Farm Limited handles your personal data.

CHOU FARM LIMITED

Chou Farm Limited is committed to respecting the privacy of visitors to our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

Our website is primarily designed to provide information about our agricultural activities, farm products, farming practices, and learning materials. We do not collect customer personal information through online order requests or enquiry forms.

2. Use of Website Information

We use our website to share information about our farm, agricultural products, farming guides, educational materials, and company activities. Information voluntarily provided through other communication channels may be used to respond to the relevant communication.

3. Cookies and Website Usage

Our website may use essential technical features or similar technologies to support its operation. If analytics or non-essential cookies are introduced, we will provide appropriate information and obtain consent where required by law.

4. Information Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information managed through our website against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or misuse. Access to website administration and farm management records is restricted to authorized personnel.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use external services to display images, maps, documents, or other content. These services may handle information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external websites they visit.

6. Privacy Rights

Visitors may contact Chou Farm Limited if they have questions or concerns about personal information associated with our website. Where applicable under Kenyan data protection law, individuals may exercise their rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or other lawful requests relating to their personal information.

7. Changes and Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website features or legal requirements change. Any revised policy will be published on this page.

For privacy-related questions, please use the official contact details published on the Chou Farm Limited website.
