Accessibility Statement

Chou Farm Limited wants everyone to be able to use this website.

Last updated: [Date to be added]

Our aim

We aim to make this website work for people using phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers, and for people who use keyboards, screen readers or other assistive tools. Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we have built in

  • A "Skip to main content" link at the start of every page.
  • Menus, buttons, forms and the photo viewer that can be used with a keyboard.
  • A clearly visible outline on whichever item has keyboard focus.
  • Labels on every form field, and clear error messages.
  • Text colours chosen for strong contrast against their backgrounds.
  • Touch targets at least 44 by 44 pixels for taps on phones.
  • Alternative text for images, entered by an administrator when a photograph is added.
  • A layout that adapts to different screen sizes and avoids sideways scrolling.
  • Reduced animation for visitors whose device is set to prefer less motion.
